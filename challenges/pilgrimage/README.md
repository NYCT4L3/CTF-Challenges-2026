# Pilgrimage

**Category:** OSINT  
**Difficulty:** Easy

## Description

A friend of mine who plays a lot of open-world crime games sent me this photo and said: "I've been to this place, sort of. A street contact once sent me on a job under it."

Find the mission. Who gives it, and what is it called?

**Flag format:** `CTF{contact_mission}`, lowercase, words separated by underscores.

Example: a contact called Big Tony and a mission called Night Shift would be `CTF{big_tony_night_shift}`.
