# Boarding Pass

**Category:** OSINT / Forensics  
**Difficulty:** Easy–Medium

## Description

A traveler posted a scan of her boarding pass online. She blacked out the sensitive parts with a marker, and even scribbled over the bigger barcode for good measure. Whatever she did not cover is true.

The flight is in 2026. Which airline is she flying, which city is she flying to, and on what date?

**Flag format:** `CTF{airline_city_date}`

- airline: full English name, lowercase, spaces as underscores
- city: lowercase English name
- date: day + 3-letter month, lowercase, e.g. `05mar`
